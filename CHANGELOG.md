# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Continuous-scroll EPUB reader with column, edge-hugging and focus-band layouts, four fonts and four backgrounds.
- OPDS library browsing and downloads, KOReader-compatible position sync, and a screen to compare and exchange places.
- Auto scroll, speed reading, bookmarks, go back through jumps, per-book speeds.
- Home carousel and shelf, tile and complications, ambient mode.
